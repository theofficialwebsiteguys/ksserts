Our Story

Built From Love, Loss, and Resilience

K'sserts began after founder Karina donated a kidney to her father, Gabriel, who was battling diabetes — and lost him just four days after his transplant. While grieving and recovering from her own surgery, she found purpose in baking desserts that everyone could enjoy, including people living with diabetes or dietary restrictions.

What started in her kitchen has grown into K'sserts Cookie Café, built on love, resilience, and inclusion — in honor of the father who changed how she sees food, and family.
