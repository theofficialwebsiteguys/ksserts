Our Story

Some Dreams Begin With Love

Some dreams begin with a business plan. Mine began with love, loss, and the belief that even life's hardest moments can inspire something beautiful.

A Father's Love

Years ago, my father, Gabriel, was battling diabetes and its devastating complications, including kidney failure. When he needed a kidney transplant, I didn't hesitate to be his donor. I told him I had a present for him — they just had to take it out of me first.

On Father's Day, in the Dominican Republic, I gave him one of my kidneys, in the hope of giving him more time with our family. Tragically, just four days after his transplant, he passed away.

Through the Darkest Days

While grieving the loss of my dad, I was also recovering from major surgery. Complications from my own recovery led to a second surgery, leaving me unable to work for nearly a year. During that difficult time, my family faced unimaginable challenges, including losing our home. It felt as though everything we had built had been taken away.

But even in the darkest moments, purpose has a way of finding us.

Finding Purpose

My father's battle with diabetes forever changed how I viewed food and the role it plays in bringing people together. I became passionate about creating desserts that everyone could enjoy—including those living with diabetes or dietary restrictions. I began developing recipes for gluten-free, vegan, and sugar-conscious treats, believing that no one should ever feel left out when it's time to celebrate.

From Kitchen to Community

What started as baking in my kitchen grew into K'sserts, a business built on love, resilience, and inclusion. Through local farmers markets, custom orders, and the incredible support of my community, I realized this dream had become much bigger than cookies.

The Next Chapter

Today, K'sserts Cookie Café is the next chapter of that journey.

My vision is to create a warm, welcoming neighborhood café where people gather over exceptional coffee, handcrafted desserts, and meaningful conversation. A place where families celebrate milestones, friends reconnect, students study, and neighbors feel at home. Whether you're looking for a classic cookie, a gluten-free dessert, a vegan treat, or a sugar-conscious option, you'll always find something made with care.

Giving Back

In honor of my father, giving back will always be part of our mission. Supporting diabetes awareness and creating desserts that bring everyone to the same table are at the heart of everything we do.

K'sserts is more than a bakery. It's proof that even after tremendous loss, hope can rise again.

Thank you for being here, for believing in this dream, and for becoming part of our story.
